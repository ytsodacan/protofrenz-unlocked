Cheek Rings
Drive small NeoPixel cheek rings off the NeoPixel connector while running a HUB75 or MAX7219 face. Set LEDs / ring to one ring's count, then pick how the two cheeks are wired — chained ring-to-ring (Series) or split from one data line with a Y-splitter (Parallel). Both make the cheeks mirror.
Your face is NeoPixel, so the cheek rings chain off the face — one ring per cheek: solder the last face module's DOUT to the left cheek's DIN, then its DOUT on to the right cheek (power both from the 5 V rail). Faces vs. Reactions

Faces are moods you choose — tap the button to move to the next Face in your rotation; it stays until you change it (or auto-returns if it has a duration).

Reactions are what happens when someone boops your snoot — the Reaction plays briefly, then your current Face comes back.

Every slot in the Editor can be a Face, a Reaction, or both — the "Used by" checkboxes decide. A slot with neither box checked is off.

The onboard LED

Solid color = your active Face's color (set per-slot in the Editor). While holding the button, the LED steps through colors — release on the color of the action you want. Rapid red blink = keep holding to exit Dance/Music.

Quick fixes

Page stuck on "Connecting"? Turn off mobile data, then open http://192.168.4.1 in your real browser (not the sign-in popup).
Home WiFi won't connect? The controller needs a 2.4 GHz network — 5 GHz-only won't work.
Forgot your suit's WiFi password? (If you set one on the ProtoFrenz hotspot:) hold the button while powering on for 3 seconds — the LED flashes orange and the network is open again.
GIF only uploads 1 frame? Make sure the file is an animated .gif — a still image has just one frame.

Full manual, guides and support: protofrenz.com (open it while connected to your home WiFi).
